Same assumptions as in [[Lemma diagonally dominant + irreducible = regular]]. Then $\forall j=1,\dots ,n:A_{jj}\neq 0$.

##### Proof:
Suppose, that $A_{jj}= 0$ for some $j$. Then $\sum_{j=1,k\neq j}^n |A_{jk}|\leq|A_{jj}|=0$.
But then $\forall k=1,\dots,n:A_{jk}=0$.
That means $A$ has a $0-$row, but this contradicts that $A$ is regular.
