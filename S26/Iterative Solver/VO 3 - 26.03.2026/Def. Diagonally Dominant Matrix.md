  - "Def.: Diagonally Dominant Matrix"
---
$A \in \mathbb{K}^n$ is called **diagonally dominant** $\iff$ $\forall j= 1,\dots,n:\sum_{k=1,j\neq k}^n|A_{jk}|\leq|A_{jj}|$ and $\exists j_{0}$ such that $\sum_{k=1,j_{0}\neq k}^n|A_{j_{0}k}|<|A_{j_{0}j_{0}}|$.

(Such a matrix does not need to be regular, like $(e_{1},0) \in \mathbb{R}^2$)
